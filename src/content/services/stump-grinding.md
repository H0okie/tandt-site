## Stump grinding vs. full removal

Tree stump grinding is the most common and cost-effective solution: our grinders reduce stumps to wood chips 6–8 inches below grade, deep enough for new sod, seed, or planting beds, and up to roughly 20 inches where hardscape needs clearance. Complete removal extracts the entire stump and root ball and is best suited for major grading, foundation prep, or new construction; it costs more and creates a larger disturbance, so we recommend it only when the project requires it, for example, before pouring a concrete pad or building a retaining wall over the stump.

We serve Mount Pleasant and East Cooper with the same equipment and standards as every Charleston job, selecting grinder sizes that fit tight access in Old Village and I'On without tearing up driveways. For land clearing, we combine grinding with brush removal, grading, and debris hauling, and grind wide, shallow live-oak surface roots to grade before backfilling to prevent settling.

## Post-stump restoration

Grinding is only half the job: the hole needs backfill, grading, and either sod, seed, or new plantings. Because we're a full-service company, you don't hire a separate contractor to finish: we remove excess chips, amend the sandy soil, lay fresh St. Augustine or Bermuda sod, and match the restored area to the surrounding grade and turf type so the repair blends in.
