Outdoor kitchen installation in Charleston, SC starts with understanding the Lowcountry: its sandy soil, salt air, high humidity, and the way people here actually use their backyards. Founded by Timothy Fowler, T&T builds custom outdoor kitchens for homeowners and commercial owners across the greater Charleston area, handling every step from design and utility coordination through construction and appliance hookup. Charleston's climate allows year-round outdoor cooking, which also means your kitchen needs materials and methods that hold up against coastal weather, UV exposure, and seasonal storms.

## Custom design-build and grill islands

Every project starts with a property walk: we assess yard layout, existing hardscape, utility access, and drainage before drawing a plan built around how you actually cook and entertain, not a generic template. Our design-build process covers structural planning, material selection, and coordination for gas, water, and electrical, plus layout decisions like L-shaped vs. linear, bar seating, and shelter options such as pergolas. Grill islands are one of our most-requested features, giving you a permanent cooking station with storage and counter space in a compact footprint, built from concrete block, natural stone, or stacked-stone veneer with steel framing, sized to your grill model, with access doors, trash pullouts, and refrigeration drawers. On tighter Mount Pleasant marshfront lots, we design to maximize usable space without crowding the yard.

## Full kitchens, countertops, and finishing

Full backyard kitchen construction goes beyond a grill island: dedicated prep areas, sinks with running water, built-in refrigerators, storage cabinets, and sometimes pizza ovens or smokers, with foundation, framing, masonry, plumbing, gas, and electrical all handled in-house. Because Charleston backyards deal with high water tables and poor drainage, we grade the pad and install proper drainage before construction so the kitchen sits level and drains correctly. For finishing, we install granite, quartz, and concrete countertops rated for outdoor use, and weather-resistant cabinetry in marine-grade polymer and stainless that won't warp, rot, or rust in salt-heavy air, plus tile backsplashes, stone veneer, and under-counter lighting.

## Appliance integration

We coordinate appliance installation and utility hookup as part of every project (built-in gas grills, side burners, warming drawers, beverage coolers, ice makers, sinks, and range hoods), with gas routing, water connections, and electrical circuits handled during the build so everything is ready when appliances arrive. We work with major outdoor appliance brands, ensure appliances sit flush, vent correctly, and meet code, and coordinate any new gas line or panel upgrade before construction so there are no mid-project delays.
