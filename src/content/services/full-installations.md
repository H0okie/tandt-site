Landscape installation in Charleston, SC requires a company that understands the Lowcountry's sandy soil, high water tables, salt exposure, and subtropical climate. Founded by Timothy Fowler and based at 2124 Parsons Corner, T&T provides full-service installation for residential and commercial properties, from initial design and grading through planting, hardscape construction, and ongoing maintenance, all under one team, with one point of contact who knows your property and the plan.

## Complete installation for new and existing properties

We install landscapes at every stage: brand-new construction lots that need a full build-out, established homes due for renovation, and commercial sites that need professional grounds from day one. For new construction, we start with grading and soil preparation (Charleston's sandy, low-lying terrain demands proper drainage planning before a single plant goes in), then handle the full sequence: grading, irrigation layout, hardscape, planting, sod, and mulching. Every project begins with a property walk to evaluate soil, drainage flow, sun and shade, and structural considerations, followed by an itemized written scope. Larger projects include a full design package with Zone 9a selections like muhly grass, palmetto palms, loropetalum, and native azaleas.

## Hardscape and softscape, sod, and garden beds

A finished landscape combines hardscape (patios, walkways, retaining walls, fire pits, outdoor kitchens, pergolas) and softscape (plants, trees, shrubs, groundcover, turf), and we install both under one crew, so there are no scheduling conflicts between separate contractors. Sod is one of our most common services: we help you choose centipede, Bermuda, St. Augustine, or Zoysia by sun, traffic, and maintenance preference, and grade every site first to prevent standing water and root rot. Our garden beds are built with proper edging, amended soil, and correct mulch depth, with plant selections matched to light conditions and your maintenance preferences.

## Mount Pleasant and commercial installs

Mount Pleasant is one of the fastest-growing areas in the metro: new subdivisions in Park West, Carolina Park, and the I-526 corridor need installations that meet HOA standards and hold up in the East Cooper climate. We install complete residential landscapes from front-yard curb-appeal packages to full backyard living spaces, and serve commercial properties along Highway 17 and Coleman Boulevard, including retail, medical offices, and multi-family developments.
