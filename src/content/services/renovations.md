Landscape renovation in Charleston, SC starts with understanding what the Lowcountry demands from a yard: sandy soil, a high water table, salt air, subtropical humidity, and hurricane seasons all shape what thrives and what fails. If your property has overgrown beds, dead turf, poor drainage, or outdated plantings, a renovation can give you a yard that works with Charleston's climate instead of fighting it. ## Complete landscape remodel

A complete remodel means starting over: we remove failing plants, rework grading and drainage, amend the soil, and install a new design from scratch: the right fit where the existing layout no longer functions from neglect, storm damage, or a landscape never designed for Charleston's flood-prone soil. Our process follows four steps: Timothy walks the property to assess goals and drainage, you receive an itemized proposal with design work, our crew executes on schedule, and we offer ongoing maintenance. Common elements include salt-tolerant and native plantings, sod replacement, hardscape additions, irrigation, and landscape lighting.

## Makeovers and restoration

A yard makeover is less intensive: refreshing what's already there by replacing struggling plants, updating bed lines, adding fresh mulch, and improving curb appeal without tearing everything out. It's ideal for homeowners preparing to sell or HOA common areas that look tired but have solid bones. Restoration addresses specific damage: after a hurricane, drought, flooding, or pest infestation, we remove dead material, correct drainage failures, replace lost plantings, and stabilize soil. Properties along tidal creeks and in low-lying West Ashley and James Island are especially prone to standing water, so we install French drains, regrade, and choose plants that handle periodic saturation.

## Garden renovation and overgrown cleanup

For Mount Pleasant garden renovations, we update perennial beds, redesign foundation plantings, or convert underperforming lawn into garden space, handling bed prep, soil amendment, planting, edging, and mulching, plus drip irrigation where needed. Overgrown yards are one of the most common reasons Charleston residents call: kudzu, privet, and wisteria can take over in a single season. Our cleanup includes brush clearing, dead tree and stump removal, shrub trimming, vine extraction, and debris hauling, after which we assess what's worth saving and present redesign options.
