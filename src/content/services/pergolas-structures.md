Pergola installation in Charleston, SC gives homeowners and businesses a functional outdoor structure that adds shade, visual appeal, and usable living space. Founded and owner-operated by Timothy Fowler, T&T builds custom pergolas and outdoor structures for residential and commercial clients across the greater Charleston area, handling every phase from the property walk-through and design through permitting, material selection, and construction. Charleston's subtropical climate makes outdoor living possible nearly year-round, and a well-built pergola extends that, delivered under one crew with one point of accountability.

## Custom design and backyard construction

Our process starts on your property: Timothy Fowler or a senior crew lead evaluates the space, discusses goals, and identifies site factors like drainage, soil, sun exposure, and existing hardscape, then produces an itemized proposal with material options. We install pergolas in pressure-treated lumber, cedar, composite, and aluminum; cedar and aluminum hold up especially well along the coast, while treated wood is a cost-effective inland option. Backyard pergolas are our most common request: freestanding designs anchor a patio, frame an outdoor kitchen, or shade a walkway, while attached designs extend from the roofline over a deck or back door. We set posts on concrete footings sized for sandy soil, use stainless or hot-dipped galvanized hardware to prevent corrosion, and can add integrated lighting, fan mounts, privacy screens, or retractable canopies.

## Arbors and outdoor structures

Arbors are smaller structures, typically placed over a garden gate, walkway entrance, or property transition: we build them in wood and metal across Mount Pleasant, Daniel Island, and East Cooper, integrating climbing supports for jasmine, Carolina jessamine, or other Lowcountry vines. Beyond pergolas and arbors, we build and renovate shade pavilions, trellises, decorative garden features, and integrated seating walls, coordinating each with hardscape, landscape design, irrigation, and lighting. Renovation is common here: older wood pergolas exposed to years of salt air and storms often need structural repairs or a full rebuild, and we give an honest recommendation: repair if it's sound, replace if it's not.

## Repair, refinishing, and maintenance

Charleston weather is hard on outdoor wood: UV fades finishes, moisture encourages mold and mildew, and storms loosen joints or crack beams. We offer repair, refinishing, and ongoing maintenance: power washing, sanding, staining or sealing, hardware inspection, and structural tightening. For clients on a recurring contract, we schedule seasonal check-ups so small issues get addressed before they become expensive problems.
