New construction landscaping in Charleston, SC starts the moment the builder clears the last piece of equipment off the lot. What's left behind (bare dirt, compacted subsoil, and construction debris mixed into the grade) needs professional attention before any grass, plants, or hardscape can go in. Founded and operated by Timothy Fowler and based at 2124 Parsons Corner, T&T works with homeowners, builders, and developers to turn raw construction sites into finished, functional landscapes, handling every phase from initial grading through final planting and irrigation, built around the sandy soil, high water tables, and salt exposure that define landscaping here.

## New home design and builder packages

A new home without landscaping is an unfinished product: we create designs based on the specific lot, accounting for sun exposure, drainage flow, soil composition, and the home's architecture, then install everything from foundation plantings and shade trees to mulch beds, lighting, and decorative features, coordinating with homeowners or builders to align with closing schedules and move-in dates. Builders need a partner who can meet deadlines across multiple lots without sacrificing quality, so we provide builder landscape packages with lot-by-lot scheduling, consistent material quality, and pace that matches construction timelines. Having worked on subdivisions from West Ashley to Mount Pleasant, we understand the HOA requirements and covenant standards that govern new development landscaping.

## Sod, grading, irrigation, and hardscape

Sod on a new site isn't as simple as rolling out turf: the subsoil is typically compacted, nutrient-poor, and mixed with debris, so we prepare the bed properly (removing rocks, aerating compacted ground, amending with topsoil) before laying warm-season varieties like St. Augustine, Bermuda, and Zoysia, each lawn getting starter fertilizer and a watering schedule for the critical first 30 days. Proper grading is the foundation of every landscape, directing water away from structures toward drainage outlets, and for multi-lot developers we handle common-area landscaping, entrance features, and retention-pond edges alongside individual lots. We install complete irrigation with rotor and spray zones, drip for beds, and smart weather-based controllers, plus a full system walkthrough. Hardscape adds structure: patios, walkways, retaining walls, fire pits, and seating in pavers, stone, and concrete, and coordinating hardscape and softscape under one company eliminates scheduling conflicts for a cohesive result.
