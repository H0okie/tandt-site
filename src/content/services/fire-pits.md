## Custom design and construction

Every property is different — lot size, existing hardscape, drainage, and how you plan to use the space all shape the right design. We start with an on-site consultation to evaluate the yard, discuss material options, and determine whether a wood-burning or gas-fueled unit fits your goals. We build custom pits using natural stone, concrete block systems, and branded pavers from manufacturers like Belgard and Tremron — round, square, rectangular, or linear, matched to your property rather than a catalog template.

## Gas and wood-burning options

Gas fire pits are popular across Mount Pleasant and East Cooper because they ignite instantly, produce no smoke, and require minimal cleanup — we route gas lines and coordinate with licensed gas fitters to meet local code, installing both natural gas (a permanent source, no refills) and propane setups where a line isn't practical. Wood-burning pits deliver the crackle and warmth many homeowners prefer, and we build them with proper airflow channels, heat-rated stone or block, and a compacted gravel-and-sand base. Charleston's sandy soil and high water table make base preparation matter more here than most markets — we excavate to depth, install drainage aggregate, and compact each layer to prevent shifting, settling, and water pooling inside the pit.

## Hardscape integration, repair, and upgrades

A fire pit works best as part of a larger outdoor living area, so we integrate construction with paver patios, sitting walls, walkways, and outdoor kitchens — frequently building a circular paver patio with a pit centered inside a low seating wall that doubles as permanent seating. We match paver colors and stone textures across the whole hardscape so it never looks bolted on. We also repair and renovate existing pits: replacing deteriorated mortar, upgrading wood-burning pits to gas burner systems, adding stone veneer over aging block, and installing wind guards or spark screens. If the bones are solid, a renovation costs far less than a full rebuild.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a fire pit in Charleston?

Most fire pit installations require local permits and compliance with fire codes. We handle permitting, gas line coordination with licensed fitters, and ensure every installation meets Charleston's requirements.

### What's the difference between gas and wood-burning fire pits?

Gas pits ignite instantly, produce no smoke, and require minimal cleanup, making them ideal for screened porches; wood-burning pits deliver crackle and smoky warmth but need more maintenance and proper airflow design.

### Why is base preparation so important here?

Charleston's sandy soil and high water table require proper excavation, drainage aggregate, and compaction to prevent shifting, settling, and water pooling after rain — a common problem with DIY installations.
