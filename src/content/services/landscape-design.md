## Custom plans for every property

Every Charleston property is different — lot shape, existing trees, soil, sun exposure, and drainage all factor into a design that works long-term. We create custom plans from a thorough property assessment, not a catalog template. The process starts with a walk-through to evaluate site conditions, discuss goals and budget, and identify constraints like easements or root systems. From there we develop a detailed plan with plants suited to the humid subtropical climate — live oaks, crepe myrtles, azaleas, camellias, yaupon holly, and muhly grass form the backbone of many residential designs.

## Functional yards and larger projects

Good design balances how a space looks with how it functions — a family with kids and dogs needs different solutions than a couple wanting a quiet garden retreat. We account for foot traffic, privacy screening, irrigation, and seasonal color, and for many properties drainage is the first problem to solve, using French drains, grading, and strategic plantings to keep water away from structures. Larger projects — full redesigns, new-construction landscapes, and commercial site plans — get detailed written scopes and phased timelines, coordinating grading, hardscape, planting, and irrigation.

## Backyard and front-yard design

Mount Pleasant homeowners increasingly treat backyards as extensions of their living space — outdoor kitchens, fire pits, pergolas, and seating areas as standard requests. We design and install these as integrated parts of a complete plan, handling hardscape, planting beds, lighting coordination, and irrigation in one scope. Out front, we design for lasting curb appeal with layered plantings, defined bed edges, and walkway integration, and we're familiar with the HOA landscape guidelines common across Mount Pleasant, Daniel Island, and West Ashley.

## Frequently asked questions

### What plants grow best in Charleston's Lowcountry climate?

Live oaks, crepe myrtles, azaleas, camellias, yaupon holly, muhly grass, palmettos, and Southern magnolias all thrive in the heat, humidity, and salt exposure. We select species based on each property's sun, drainage, and soil so they establish well across all four seasons.

### How long does a landscape design project take?

It depends on scope — a front-yard refresh can often be done within a few weeks of approval, while larger jobs involving grading, drainage, hardscape, and irrigation may run several weeks to a few months, with phased timelines.

### Do you handle drainage as part of the design?

Yes — drainage is often the first issue we solve, since high water tables and heavy summer rain can ruin turf and beds. We build French drains, grading, swales, and strategic plantings into the design to protect everything installed afterward.

### Do you offer maintenance after installation?

Yes — recurring maintenance contracts handled by the same team that built the space, including seasonal pruning, bed maintenance, fertilization, and adjustments as plants mature.
