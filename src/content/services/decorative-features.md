## Decorative rock and boulder placement

Decorative rock adds texture, color, and low-maintenance ground cover — we install gravel beds, river rock borders, crushed granite pathways, and stone mulch that reduce water usage and hold up against heavy summer rains. It works especially well around foundation plantings, along driveways, and where traditional mulch washes out on slopes, and every installation includes proper landscape fabric and edging to keep rock in place and prevent weeds. Popular choices are warm sandstone, grey granite, and coastal white stone. Boulders serve as natural anchor points — we source and place them in varying sizes for retaining features, garden borders, and standalone focal points, frequently using them to manage grade changes on sloped lots and to define transitions between lawn and beds.

## Accents, lighting, and focal points

Small details make the biggest difference — we install decorative stepping stones, seat walls, garden edging, brick borders, and custom planter surrounds that tie together larger hardscape projects, plus low-voltage lighting to highlight stonework, trees, and architecture after dark. Charleston's traditional wrought-iron aesthetic pairs well with lantern-style path lights and subtle uplighting on a live oak canopy. A focal point gives a garden structure: we install fountains, water bowls, statuary, large decorative urns, and custom raised beds as centerpieces. In Mount Pleasant neighborhoods like I'On, Old Village, and Park West, a recirculating courtyard fountain fits a Charleston-style garden while masking street noise, and basin features attract local wildlife.

## Layered ornamental landscapes

Beyond individual installations, we create layered ornamental landscapes that combine multiple elements into a cohesive design — pairing decorative rock with native plantings like muhly grass, azaleas, and crape myrtles, and integrating pergolas, arbors, and trellises with climbing jasmine. For commercial properties we improve entrances, common areas, and parking lot islands, and HOA communities across Daniel Island, James Island, and West Ashley rely on us for consistent, professional-grade ornamental landscaping that maintains property values.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best decorative features for Charleston's climate?

Salt-air-rated stone accents, native plantings like muhly grass and crape myrtles, recirculating water features, and low-voltage lighting — all selected for humidity, UV exposure, and subtropical conditions.

### How do you handle drainage for water features?

We account for the high water table when installing fountains, evaluating site-specific drainage during the initial property walk and designing systems that prevent waterlogging, with grading tailored to sandy soils.

### Why do decorative rock and stone mulch work well here?

They reduce water usage, hold up against heavy summer rains, and prevent erosion on slopes where traditional mulch washes out — with warm sandstone, grey granite, and coastal white stone requiring minimal maintenance.

### Do you provide maintenance after installation?

Yes — ongoing contracts cover rock-bed refreshing, lighting bulb replacement, seasonal plant care, and regular inspections to protect your investment through every Lowcountry season.
