## Lawn insect control

Charleston's subtropical climate supports a wide range of turf-damaging insects: mole crickets tunnel through sandy soil and destroy roots, webworms leave brown thinning patches, and fleas and ticks establish in turf. We provide surface and subsurface treatments that target active infestations and prevent new ones, using products matched to your grass type (Bermuda, Zoysia, centipede, or St. Augustine) and applied at the right point in the pest's life cycle.

## Fire ant and grub control

Fire ants are among the most aggressive Lowcountry pests — a single colony can hold hundreds of thousands of ants and produce new mounds quickly after spring and fall rains. We combine broadcast bait treatments (preventive) with individual mound treatments (fast knockdown of active colonies), plus seasonal follow-ups since fire ants migrate from neighboring properties.

Grubs — the larval stage of Japanese beetles and June bugs — feed on grass roots and make turf feel spongy and pull up easily. We apply preventive grub control in late spring and early summer before egg hatch, and curative treatments where grubs are already established. Left untreated, grubs also attract armadillos and birds that dig up lawns.

## Chinch bugs and Mount Pleasant conditions

Chinch bugs target St. Augustine — one of Charleston's most popular grasses — piercing blades, injecting a toxin, and creating spreading yellow-to-brown patches that homeowners often mistake for drought stress. We inspect the turf canopy, especially along heat-radiating sidewalks and driveways where they concentrate, then apply targeted treatments and monitor for re-infestation. Mount Pleasant properties near tidal creeks see higher flea and mosquito pressure, and newer sod on Daniel Island and in I'On is more vulnerable during establishment.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the most common lawn pests in Charleston?

Fire ants, grubs, chinch bugs, mole crickets, and webworms. Fire ants are especially aggressive; grubs feed on roots; chinch bugs damage St. Augustine by injecting toxins. Each needs targeted treatment.

### When is the best time to treat grubs?

Late spring through early summer, before egg hatch — grubs feed below the surface before damage is visible. If an infestation is already present, curative treatments reduce populations and protect remaining turf.

### Can I treat chinch bugs myself?

Professional treatment is recommended, because chinch bug damage is easily mistaken for drought stress — which delays control and lets the infestation spread. We identify activity through close inspection and time treatments to the warm months when they're most active.
