Sprinkler system installation in Charleston, SC starts with understanding the ground beneath your feet. Sandy Lowcountry soil drains fast, so water moves through the root zone before most turf grasses can absorb it. Add salt exposure, a high water table that shifts with the tides, and summer heat above 90°F for weeks straight, and you need a properly designed system, not a few pop-up heads on a timer. Founded and operated by Timothy Fowler, T&T designs systems around the specific conditions on your property (soil type, sun, plant material, grade, and water pressure) so every zone delivers the right amount of water exactly where it's needed.

## Residential and commercial installation

A residential system keeps your lawn and beds watered on a consistent schedule without dragging hoses or waiting on rain that may not come for weeks. We size each zone from pressure readings taken at your meter, then select heads (rotary nozzles for turf, spray heads for smaller strips, drip emitters for beds) that deliver even coverage without overspray onto sidewalks, and most standard-lot installs take one to three days. Commercial properties have different demands: HOA common areas, storefronts, office parks, and multi-family complexes need high-output rotor heads, master valves, flow sensors, and dedicated backflow assemblies that meet Charleston's municipal code, scheduled around tenant activity and business hours.

## Design, drip, and upgrades

Good irrigation starts on paper: before we trench a line, we map the property for head-to-head coverage, matched precipitation rates, and separate zones for turf versus beds, with cycle-and-soak scheduling built in to prevent runoff on fast-draining sandy soil, plus attention to shade, slope, and distance from foundations. Drip irrigation delivers water straight to the root zone of shrubs, perennials, and trees, using 30–50% less water than spray heads and keeping foliage dry to reduce fungal disease; we run drip lines under mulch with pressure regulators, inline emitter tubing, and filters. For older systems, we retrofit fixed-spray heads that outpace sandy soil with high-efficiency rotary nozzles, and replace cracked PVC, corroded valves, and outdated controllers.

## Smart controllers

Smart controllers adjust watering based on local weather, soil moisture, and seasonal changes: we install WiFi-enabled controllers from Hunter Hydrawise, Rachio, and Rain Bird that you monitor from your phone. In Charleston, where an afternoon thunderstorm can drop an inch of rain in 30 minutes, a smart controller skips the next cycle automatically, saving water and preventing overwatering, which is just as damaging to Lowcountry turf as underwatering.
