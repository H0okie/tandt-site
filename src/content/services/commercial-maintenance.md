## Maintenance and office landscaping

Consistent maintenance keeps a commercial property looking professional year-round — we provide scheduled programs that include pruning, mulching, bed care, seasonal color rotations, and debris removal, built around your property's plant types, foot-traffic patterns, and the Lowcountry's long growing season. Contracts cover everything from weekly site visits to quarterly deep-care services, giving property managers and HOA boards a single point of contact and crews who know the property layout without direction. For office parks and professional buildings along Highway 17 and the East Cooper corridor, we handle entrance plantings, parking lot islands, walkway borders, and seasonal flower beds — a well-kept landscape signals professionalism to clients and tenants before they reach the front door.

## Enhancement and groundskeeping

Beyond routine care, we handle full landscape design and installation for commercial properties — hardscape like decorative walkways, retaining walls, and outdoor seating, plus irrigation, French drains, and stormwater management. For retail centers, restaurants, and hospitality properties, we build outdoor living features like fire pits, pergolas, and decorative accents that create inviting customer spaces, following a clear four-step process: property assessment, written proposal, execution, and ongoing maintenance. Complete groundskeeping goes beyond mowing to cover tree trimming, storm cleanup, pressure washing, irrigation repair, and new-construction installs — we work with property management companies, builders, and real estate professionals who need reliable recurring site care, handle storm debris removal quickly during hurricane season, and coordinate long-term improvement plans that phase in upgrades without disrupting tenants.
