## Backyard seating and patio construction

A backyard seating area starts with understanding how you actually use the space — a quiet coffee spot, a family dinner area, or a multi-zone layout with separate dining and lounging. Our installations typically include paver or natural stone patios, built-in seating walls, and integrated planting beds. Patio construction is the foundation of most projects, built in concrete pavers, travertine, flagstone, or stamped concrete: we excavate to the correct depth, install geotextile fabric where needed, and compact crushed stone in lifts. Without that step, pavers shift and settle unevenly within a year. Low seating walls of matching block or stone double as retaining features on sloped lots and provide permanent, low-maintenance seating.

## Lounges, garden seating, and entertainment build-outs

For a relaxed lounge setup, we build with recessed fire pits, wide-format pavers, and open layouts for deep-seating furniture — and near the coast we use stainless anchors, marine-grade fasteners, and UV-resistant finishes as standard, plus low-voltage lighting to extend usability into the evening. Garden seating areas blend a small stone or paver pad with structured beds, ornamental grasses, and shrubs, with shade trees positioned to block afternoon sun and fragrant plants like gardenias near the seating zone. For a complete outdoor living space, our entertainment build-outs combine seating with an outdoor kitchen island, fire feature, pergola, and dedicated dining and lounging zones — we manage hardscape, gas and electrical rough-in coordination, countertop fabrication, and landscape finishing, typically running four to eight weeks depending on scope and permits.

## Frequently asked questions

### What makes seating area installation in Charleston different?

Sandy soil, a high water table, salt-air exposure, and humidity require specialized base preparation, material selection, and drainage planning. Generic methods fail quickly here, so local expertise ensures the seating area lasts for years.

### What materials work best in Charleston's climate?

Concrete pavers, travertine, and flagstone all perform well when set on a properly compacted base; near the coast we favor products that resist fading, cracking, and moisture damage, paired with marine-grade hardware and UV-resistant finishes.

### How long does it take to build a seating area?

Simple patio seating areas may take 1–2 weeks, while full entertainment areas with kitchens and fire features typically run 4–8 weeks. You receive a detailed schedule during the proposal stage.

### Can I add an outdoor kitchen or fire pit?

Yes — we build complete entertainment areas combining seating with cooking and fire features, handling hardscape, gas and electrical coordination, and landscape finishing under one point of contact.
