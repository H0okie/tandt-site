## Lawn mowing in Charleston, SC

Every mowing visit includes cutting to the proper height for your turf type, edging along driveways, sidewalks, and landscape beds, line trimming around obstacles, and blowing debris off all hard surfaces.

Charleston lawns are predominantly warm-season grasses: St. Augustine, Bermuda, Centipede, and Zoysia, and each has a specific ideal mowing height. St. Augustine performs best at 3.5 to 4 inches, while Bermuda should stay between 1 and 2 inches. Cutting too low scalps the turf and invites weed pressure, so our crews set blade heights based on your specific grass, not a universal setting.

We follow the one-third rule on every visit: never remove more than one-third of the grass blade in a single cut. This reduces plant stress, promotes deeper root growth, and keeps your lawn thick enough to crowd out weeds naturally.

## Weekly lawn mowing in Charleston

During peak growing season (roughly April through October), warm-season grasses can grow an inch or more per week. Weekly mowing keeps your lawn at the correct height without shock-cutting overgrown turf. We offer weekly mowing contracts that lock in your day and time, with priority scheduling after storms or heavy rain. For the slower cooler months, we adjust to biweekly service from November through early March, so you only pay for what you need.

## One-time grass cutting

Need a single visit instead of a recurring plan? We handle one-time mowing for homeowners preparing a property for sale, landlords between tenants, or anyone who fell behind during a rainy stretch. One-time cuts include the same full service: mow, edge, trim, and blow. For badly overgrown lawns we may make multiple passes at decreasing heights to avoid scalping, and we'll tell you upfront if extra time is needed and what it will cost.
