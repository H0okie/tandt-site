## Complete synthetic turf installation

Every install starts with an on-site assessment of soil composition, drainage, and sun exposure before we recommend a product and base system. We remove existing sod or soil, grade for drainage, compact a crushed-stone base, lay a weed barrier, and install the turf with precision seaming and infill. In Charleston, drainage prep matters more than most places — the high water table and frequent heavy rain mean a poorly built base fails within a season, so we build every base with that in mind. Projects range from small side yards to full front-and-back replacements, plus commercial installs for office parks, retail, and multi-family common areas.

## Artificial grass for gardens, patios, pools, and play areas

Want to keep garden beds and hardscape borders but swap the grass? We fit artificial grass around flower beds, palms, shrubs, and stone edging. Around pools it stays cooler than concrete and eliminates tracked-in mud; on patios it drains fast after a summer storm. For play areas we install shock-absorbing underlayment that meets ASTM fall-height safety standards — popular on James Island and in West Ashley where families spend time outdoors.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does artificial turf last in Charleston's climate?

Quality turf on a proper base with UV-resistant fibers lasts 15 to 20 years here. Base construction is the key factor — poor drainage shortens turf life significantly, so we build every base for Charleston's rainfall and high water table.

### Does artificial turf get too hot in Charleston summers?

It can get warm in direct July–August sun. Newer products with lighter fibers and heat-reflective technology cut surface temperatures by up to 15%, and shaded or poolside areas stay comfortable. We help you choose based on how much sun your yard gets.

### Do I need HOA approval for artificial turf?

Many area HOAs now allow it, but requirements vary — neighborhoods like Park West and Dunes West have specific standards. We recommend submitting a turf sample and installation plan to your board, and we can provide product specs to support the request.
