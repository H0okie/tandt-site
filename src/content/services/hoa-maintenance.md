## Maintenance, grounds care, and lawn care

Consistent maintenance keeps property values up and complaints down — we provide scheduled mowing, edging, blowing, and bed maintenance on a weekly or bi-weekly cycle, with crews following set routes and checklists so every common area, entrance, and amenity gets the same attention each visit. Programs are built around the Lowcountry's growing calendar: warm-season grasses like Bermuda, Zoysia, and St. Augustine need different mowing heights and fertilization timing than upstate turf, so we adjust to actual conditions rather than a corporate template. Grounds care covers everything beyond turf — parking areas, sidewalk edging, storm debris, leaf cleanup, pressure washing of common hardscapes, and litter pickup — all under one contract. After hurricanes, our crews prioritize debris removal and damaged-tree assessment, with the equipment for fast emergency cleanup and insurance documentation. Lawn care adds aeration, overseeding, fertilization, weed control, and insect management tuned to sandy, acidic soils and Charleston's fungal-disease and chinch-bug pressure.

## Design, seasonal color, irrigation, and tree care

We design and install features for common areas, clubhouse surrounds, pool decks, entry monuments, and streetscapes, selecting Zone 8b/9a performers like muhly grass, dwarf palmetto, and Southern wax myrtle that handle salt and periodic flooding, with full drawings and written scope on larger projects. First impressions matter, so we rotate seasonal color at entries and high-visibility beds — lantana, pentas, and SunPatiens for spring and summer; pansies, ornamental kale, and cyclamen for fall and winter — and maintain permanent beds with fresh mulch and defined edging. We install, repair, and manage irrigation (controller programming, head adjustments, leak detection, seasonal shutoff, drip and rain sensors), and perform full audits on aging systems to lower water costs. For tree and shrub care, we handle trimming, crown reduction, hazard-limb and full removal with stump grinding on palms, live oaks, and crepe myrtles, plus screening-hedge rotations aligned to each species' growth cycle.
