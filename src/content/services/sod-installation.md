## Full-service residential and commercial sod installation

Our process starts with a property walk where we assess soil quality, grading, drainage, and sun exposure, then prepare a detailed, itemized quote. Our crews handle every phase: removing old turf, correcting grade issues, amending Charleston's sandy soil with organic material, laying sod in tight seams, and rolling the finished surface for full soil contact. We install warm-season varieties suited to the Lowcountry — St. Augustine, Bermuda, Zoysia, and Centipede — and finish with watering instructions specific to your yard.

## New sod for construction and bare yards

New construction sites and bare lots present unique challenges — builder-grade soil is often compacted fill with poor nutrient content and limited drainage. We grade the yard to direct water away from the foundation, amend the top layer of soil, and lay fresh sod the same day it arrives, because sod left on a pallet in Charleston's summer heat deteriorates within hours.

## Sod replacement

Dead patches, fungal damage, chinch bug infestations, and years of compaction can leave a lawn beyond recovery. Sod replacement gives you a clean reset without the months-long wait of seeding. We remove the old turf, address the underlying cause of failure — poor drainage, compaction, shade, or pests — and install fresh sod matched to your conditions. Where St. Augustine has failed in heavy shade, we may recommend Zoysia or a shade-tolerant cultivar instead of repeating the same mistake.

## Precision sod laying

The difference between a good sod job and a great one is in the details. Gaps invite weeds, overlapping edges create uneven surfaces, and poor soil contact keeps roots from reaching moisture. Our crews lay sod in a staggered brick pattern with tight seams, roll every section for firm contact, and water immediately. We pay close attention to edges along walkways and beds, and on sloped yards we stake sod to prevent movement during heavy Lowcountry rain until roots take hold.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best month to install sod in Charleston?

April through September for warm-season grasses like St. Augustine, Bermuda, and Zoysia — they root fastest in warm soil, and spring installs get the longest season to establish before winter.

### How long does new sod take to root?

Typically 10 to 14 days with correct watering, reaching full establishment in three to six weeks. Daily watering during the first two weeks keeps edges from drying out in the heat.

### Why is soil preparation so important here?

Charleston's sandy soil doesn't retain moisture or nutrients well. Amending with organic material, correcting drainage, and grading away from foundations is what lets roots establish and grass stay healthy long-term.
