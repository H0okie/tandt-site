## Lawn and yard leveling

Leveling corrects bumps, dips, and uneven surfaces that make mowing difficult, create tripping hazards, and pool water in the wrong places. We start with a grade assessment, then add or redistribute soil (typically a sand-and-compost blend suited to Charleston's base) and smooth the surface for even water distribution. Low spots near foundations are common here due to settling and the high water table; leveling directs water away from structures and reduces standing water that breeds mosquitoes and fungus. Yard leveling extends this to the whole outdoor space, grading around patios, walks, and beds so stormwater flows to proper drainage points.

## Lawn restoration

Restoration is the right call when grass is thin, patchy, or weed-overrun but the soil base is still workable. Rather than tearing everything out, we dethatch, aerate, amend the soil, and overseed or plug with warm-season grasses (St. Augustine, Bermuda, Zoysia, or Centipede) based on sun and traffic. We also identify what caused the decline (compaction, improper watering, fungal infections like large patch, or chinch bug damage) because fixing the symptom without the cause just repeats the problem next season.

## Lawn repair and regrading

For newer Mount Pleasant developments, we handle spot sodding, soil amendment after construction or utility work, and repair of irrigation dead spots; in established neighborhoods shaded by mature live oaks, we select shade-tolerant varieties. Regrading is a more involved reshaping of slope and contour to fix persistent flooding or water pooling near the foundation, establishing at least a 1% grade away from structures. We coordinate regrading with French drains or catch basins when needed and can lay sod or seed immediately after.
