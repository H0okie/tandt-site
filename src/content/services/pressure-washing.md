## Residential washing and driveways

Charleston homeowners deal with algae, mold, and mildew on nearly every exterior surface — humidity, warm temperatures, and coastal moisture make these organisms grow fast and return quickly without proper cleaning. Our residential service covers exterior walls, walkways, fences, garage doors, and any hard surface, adjusting pressure to the material: high PSI for concrete and masonry, soft-wash methods for painted wood, vinyl, and stucco, with pre-treatment solutions that break down organic growth before washing. Driveways take the most visible beating — tire marks, oil stains, algae streaks, and ground-in dirt — so we use surface cleaners and high-pressure equipment for even coverage without the streaking handheld wands leave behind, cleaning paver joints carefully to avoid displacing sand and re-sanding and sealing after washing if needed.

## House, patio, deck, and commercial

Full-house exterior washing removes the green and black streaking Charleston's climate produces on siding, fascia, soffits, and trim — one of our most-requested services and a dramatic single-visit difference. For most homes we use a soft-wash approach: a low-pressure stream with biodegradable solution that kills algae and mildew at the root, safer for vinyl and HardiePlank than high pressure and longer-lasting because the organisms are killed rather than just blasted off. Patios and decks see heavy weathering, so we match pressure and solution to each material — low-pressure wash plus brightener for wood decks, full high-pressure for concrete — and clean around outdoor kitchens, fire pits, and seating areas that collect grease and organic buildup. For businesses, we power wash retail centers, office buildings, restaurants, HOA common areas, and multi-family properties, offering one-time deep cleans and recurring monthly or quarterly contracts, often scheduling early-morning or after-hours work to avoid disrupting operations.

## Frequently asked questions

### What's the best time of year to pressure wash a house here?

Spring and early fall are ideal — a wash in March through May removes winter buildup before peak humidity, and a second wash in October clears residue from summer storms and pollen season.

### Do you offer soft washing as well as pressure washing?

Yes — soft washing uses lower pressure with specialized solutions to safely clean vinyl siding, stucco, painted surfaces, and roofing. We assess each surface and choose the right method: high pressure for concrete and brick, soft wash for delicate materials.

### How long does a typical job take?

A standard driveway takes about 30 to 60 minutes; a full-house exterior wash usually takes two to four hours depending on size and the amount of algae or mildew. Commercial properties vary by square footage and surface type.

### Does pressure washing damage concrete or siding?

Not when done correctly — damage comes from too much pressure on the wrong surface or holding the nozzle too close. Our crews match PSI and nozzle tips to each material: concrete and pavers handle high pressure well, while siding and wood decks get a gentler approach with appropriate cleaning agents.
