T&T provides professional water feature installation in Charleston, SC for residential and commercial properties throughout the Lowcountry: a fountain at your front entry, a pondless waterfall in the backyard, or a custom stone feature built into an outdoor living space, with design, installation, and ongoing maintenance handled by our team. Founded by Timothy Fowler and based at 2124 Parsons Corner, T&T builds around the specific conditions here: sandy soil, high water tables, salt exposure, and a subtropical climate that keeps outdoor spaces in use nearly year-round.

## Fountains and backyard water features

We install prebuilt and custom fountains for front yards, courtyards, entryways, and commercial properties (pedestal, tiered, and wall-mounted styles), handling site prep, plumbing, electrical, basin work, and surrounding hardscape, with each unit set on a stable, level foundation for sandy soil. A backyard water feature can be as simple as a bubbling rock or as involved as a multi-tier waterfall feeding a natural pond: we start with a walkthrough to evaluate grade, drainage, sun exposure, and existing landscape, then handle excavation, liner installation, pump and filtration, stone placement, and planting so the result looks like it belongs on your property, not dropped in from a catalog.

## Pondless and custom features

Pondless water features are one of our most popular requests across Mount Pleasant and East Cooper: they circulate water over rocks into a hidden underground reservoir, giving you the look and sound of flowing water without an open pond, which is lower maintenance, safer for young children and pets, and ideal for smaller yards. We install pondless waterfalls, disappearing fountains, and bubbling rock systems with properly sized basins, commercial-grade pumps, and natural stone. For projects that don't fit a template, Timothy Fowler works directly with clients on custom builds (a stream through a garden bed, a stone spillway integrated into a retaining wall, or a feature built into an outdoor kitchen), with a written scope, materials list, and timeline before any ground is broken. Near Daniel Island, Isle of Palms, or Folly Beach, we factor salt-air corrosion resistance into material and pump selection.

## Repair and maintenance

Existing water features need regular service: we provide pump replacement, liner repair, filtration cleaning, algae treatment, and full system diagnostics for fountains, ponds, and pondless features. If your feature was installed by another company and has stopped working or is leaking, we assess it and give an honest repair-or-replace recommendation. Our seasonal maintenance contracts cover spring startup, mid-season cleaning, fall leaf protection, and winter shutdown procedures specific to Charleston's mild but unpredictable climate.
